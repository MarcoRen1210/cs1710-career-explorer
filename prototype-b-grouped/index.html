How to read the petals
  • Distance from center = salary (rings every $100K, $0 at the center circle)
  • Petal width = density of salaries at that level (scaled to each role's peak)
  • Dots = individual salary records; white segment = middle 50% (IQR); white dot = median
  • Salaries above $400K sit just outside the outer ring
  • Categories are ordered clockwise by median salary; “Other” last
  • Selected categories take their comparison color (① cyan, ② amber)
