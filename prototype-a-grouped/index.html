    Career category ↔ BLS occupation mapping and data sources

    Salary data: salaries_grouped.csv, filtered to employee_residence = US and work_year = 2024, using salary_in_usd and career_category. Self-reported; all job titles and employment types included. The full title → category list is in data/job_title_to_career_category_mapping.csv.

    BLS data: bls_career_outlook_grouped.csv. OEWS median annual wages (2025) and Employment Projections (2025–2035). Aggregate official statistics for whole occupations; not directly comparable with individual self-reported salaries.
